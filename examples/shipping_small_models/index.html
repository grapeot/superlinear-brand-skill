The recipe

Four steps from a large teacher to a small student

  1. 01Write the eval firstA hundred real inputs with the answers you would accept
  2. 02Collect the teacher’s answersRun the large model on thousands of task inputs
  3. 03Distil into the studentFine-tune the small model on those answers
  4. 04Quantise, then measure againSame eval, on the target device

A working rule
Pick the smallest model that passes your eval, then stop shopping.
Rule of thumbUsed throughout this example deck
